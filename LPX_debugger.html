Launchpad X — Control Deck

A minimal offline panel for talking to a Launchpad X over Web MIDI. Connect the device, pick a lighting mode and colour, then click pads to send real Note On messages — or watch incoming presses in the log. Requires Chrome or Edge (Web MIDI isn't supported in Firefox or Safari).
